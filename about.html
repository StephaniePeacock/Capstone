About The Princess Posse

As the only official fan club of her royal highness Grand Champion, Breed Winner Regional, National Winner Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk, the Princess Posse is dedicated to celebrating and supporting her throughout her crawl and beyond. Together we have helped not only in funding her royal adventures, but also been instrumental in several plans aimed at vexing those who would seek to harm her.
