Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Princess Donut?
She is a prize-winning tortoiseshell Persian show cat (referred to as a "Queen Anne Chonk") who originally belonged to Carl's ex-girlfriend, Bea. When the world ends, Carl rescues her and brings her into the alien dungeon as Crawler #4,119.
How does she learn to talk and get powers?
She enters the dungeon as a pet, but after grabbing and eating an Enhanced Pet Biscuit from a Legendary Pet Box, she gains sapience, human-level intelligence, and the ability to speak.
What are her main abilities?
She gains magical spells, charisma-based skills, and signature combat moves—including the fan-favorite ability to shoot lasers from her eyes.
What is her role in the party?
Donut considers herself the true leader and "Grand Champion Best in Dungeon" of the Royal Court of Princess Donut, officially designating Carl as her "Royal Bodyguard". She also later adopts a pet velociraptor named Mongo.
Why is she so good at the game?
As a professional show cat, she already understands performance, ego, and cameras. She quickly realizes that surviving the alien reality show requires making "exquisite television" to win fan favor and sponsorships.
Why is the Princess Posse the best fan club?
It's the only official fan club! As an officially licensed fan club, the Princess Posse's proceeds are used to support Princess Donut's journey throughout her crawl. The Donut Holes, as an unofficial fan club, do not contribute a dime to our beloved Princess Donut, and actively steal funds that she needs!
How often does the Princess Posse meet?
It varies! Local chapters decide their own schedules. We host an annual convention for all members, and charity events are scheduled throughout the year.
